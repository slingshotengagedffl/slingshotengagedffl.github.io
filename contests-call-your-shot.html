How It Works
  • You get one shot per season. Use it on a week you're confident — or a week you need to make a statement.
  • Once you call it, the page locks for you. Everyone in the league sees your call on the home page in real time.
  • Win that week's matchup and you collect an extra $5 from the league pot. Lose it and your opponent gets the $5 instead. The $5 is already baked into the buy-in — no one is settling up directly.
