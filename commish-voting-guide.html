Prop 1
# of Playoff Teams
👍Yes Strong Yes
2025 Rule
Seven of 14 teams made the playoffs in 2025, a one-year departure from the traditional six-team field. Only the #1 seed received a first-round bye.
2026 Proposal
Adopt the seven-team playoff field as the new standard going forward.
🎙️ The Commish's Take

Seven teams gives real incentive to earn the #1 seed, since it's the only bye — with six teams, two clubs got one. More teams in the mix for a title is more exciting, and this format doesn't need to be so serious. The NFL runs seven per conference, and the NBA takes more than half its league to the playoffs.

Prop 2  ·  🔥 Strongest Feeling of All 10
Bonus Wins and Losses
👍Yes Strong Yes
2025 Rule
Owners earn exactly one win or one loss each week against their designated head-to-head opponent.
2026 Proposal
Try ESPN's "Bonus Wins and Losses" feature for one season, applied only to playoff seeding — not official league records:
  • Score in the league's top half for the week → extra win
  • Score in the bottom half → extra loss
This matters more in 2026's "Fantasy Classic" format, where seeding goes by record first and total points as the tiebreaker.
🎙️ The Commish's Take

This balances out schedule luck — if you post the second-best score of the week but lose to the best score, you go 1-1 instead of 0-1 in the standings. It's a common feature among fantasy players, and I've heard it makes Sunday and Monday night games more interesting since more teams have something at stake trying to crack the top half. We can try it for one year in this simpler seeding format and see if we like it. Of all ten proposals, I feel strongest about this one.

Prop 3
Lineup Protection
👍Yes Mild Yes
2025 Rule
Players are locked into a lineup at the kickoff time of their game.
2026 Proposal
Turn on ESPN's "Lineup Protection" feature, which automatically replaces a starter listed as Out or on IR with an eligible bench player who hasn't played yet. Per ESPN's own description, the replacement is their highest-ranked eligible player for that roster spot — not necessarily the highest-projected one.
🎙️ The Commish's Take

I don't feel strongly either way on this one. It would ease situations where a player is ruled out unexpectedly close to kickoff, or where an owner can't log in in time to make a swap. It's a small win for competitive fairness since no one accidentally starts an inactive player, and I don't see a real downside.

Prop 5
Waiver Tiebreaker
👍Yes Mild Yes
2025 Rule
Each week, ties for the highest FAAB bid go to the team lower in the standings.
2026 Proposal
Change the tiebreaker method to "Move to Last After Claim, Never Reset." Any time a team wins a FAAB bid, they move to the bottom of the tiebreaker order. Standings no longer factor in.
🎙️ The Commish's Take

This seems simpler than resetting based on standings — it's a straightforward rotation that isn't tied to how teams are performing. As simple as that.

Prop 6
FAAB Boost for Playoff Teams
👎No Mild No
2025 Rule
Everyone starts the season with $100 FAAB. FAAB can be traded. Half the league wins an additional $20 FAAB for winning the Ryder Cup.
2026 Proposal
In addition to the above, teams that reach the playoffs receive an additional $20 FAAB at the end of the regular season to help them in the playoffs.
🎙️ The Commish's Take

Saving FAAB for the playoffs is already a strategy choice teams can make on their own. Non-playoff teams are still contending for something — the Hottest Team in Sports Bowl, and avoiding the Bronze Franz — so I don't think playoff teams need an extra boost.

Prop 7
Money in the Bank
👍Yes Strong Yes
2025 Rule
The AVL Memorial Cup Tournament (AVLMCT) winner receives Prestige, a cash prize, and the #1 pick in the Week 14 schedule draft.
2026 Proposal
In honor of WWE's "Money in the Bank," the AVLMCT winner earns one perk: once during the season, they can steal a player another owner won on waivers after waivers clear.
  • The AVLMCT winner must have personally bid at least $1 on the player being stolen (no sniping a player they never wanted)
  • They pay the player's original winning bid, plus $1
  • The owner who originally won the player — and now loses them — has their FAAB refunded
  • Must be exercised by noon Pacific on the day waivers clear
🎙️ The Commish's Take

This is just fun — more chaos, more strategy, more reason to care what your rivals are bidding on waivers. It's a one-time event each season, so it's not a huge competitive swing.

Prop 8
Intercontinental Championship Bounty
👍Yes Moderate Yes
2025 Rule
The Intercontinental Championship transfers between owners throughout the season based on head-to-head results. Holding the belt at the end of Week 14 is a matter of prestige but carries no cash prize.
2026 Proposal
The IC belt now carries a cash bounty that grows $5 each week:
  • Belt changes hands (champion loses their matchup) → new champion immediately collects the accumulated pot, bounty resets to $0
  • Whoever holds the belt at the end of Week 14 collects whatever has built up since the belt last changed hands
  • Maximum possible payout if the belt never changes hands all season: $70
🎙️ The Commish's Take

Adds a little extra excitement to the Intercontinental race. I don't see a real downside.

Prop 9
Keeper Eligibility & Trade Deadline
👍Yes Strong Yes
2025 Rule
A player must be on your roster prior to the trade deadline, and held through Week 17, to be keeper-eligible.
2026 Proposal
Drop the trade-deadline requirement entirely. Keeper eligibility would be based solely on being on your roster at the end of Week 17. Cost would still be determined by the existing draft-position and waiver rules.
🎙️ The Commish's Take

The deadline rule exists to stop teams from dropping useful players late to stash cheap keepers. But the cheapest possible keeper cost is already a 4th-round pick, and almost nothing on waivers in December is worth that next year. It protects against a risk that barely exists, and cutting it makes keeper tracking way simpler.

Prop 10
Keeper Eligibility Locked on Draft Day
👍Yes Moderate Yes
2025 Rule
Any player who spent time on waivers during the season costs a flat 4th-round pick to keep the following season. Exception: players originally drafted in rounds 3–5 cost their draft round minus two (a 3rd-round pick costs a 1st, a 4th costs a 2nd, a 5th costs a 3rd).
2026 Proposal
Lock every player's keeper cost in on draft day, even if they're later dropped and re-added off waivers:
  • Drafted rounds 1–2: not keeper-eligible
  • Drafted rounds 3–14: cost = draft round minus two
  • Undrafted / added off waivers: flat 4th-round pick
  • Kept a 2nd consecutive year: flat 1st-round pick
🎙️ The Commish's Take

This is about simplicity — every player's keeper cost is locked in on draft day, which makes it far easier to sort out keepers at the end of the season. It also creates more desirable keeper options, since a player drafted late, dropped, and made valuable again can still be scooped up at their original cost. That adds more strategy and more fun to the format.
