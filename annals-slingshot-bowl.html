Cousins, unvaccinated, caught COVID and missed the Vikings-Packers game. Jon's argument is straightforward: Cousins plays, the game is closer, Jones gets more carries, Jon wins. Dylan got the Daddy. Jordan hung the * Chain. The debate never ended.

📖 Lore

Alex V. went 12-1 in the regular season, dubbed the Luck King for a campaign built more on favorable schedules than dominant performances. Jack's team was unspectacular by comparison — his MVP was DeVante Parker, a mid-season trade acquisition who had bounced through two other rosters before landing in Jack's lineup. Jack became the first back-to-back champion and the first owner to win two titles, while Alex V. pocketed the Luck King title and little else.

📖 Lore

The two highest-scoring teams in league history played each other in the same championship game. Those records still stand eight years later. Kyle's squad set the all-time scoring record; Jack's finished second all-time. Kyle was the heavy favorite. Jack had Aaron Rodgers — acquired at the trade deadline — and Rodgers' teammate Davante Adams, and that was enough. A 142-point performance gave Jack his second title and cemented Bowl V as the high-water mark of Slingshot football.
