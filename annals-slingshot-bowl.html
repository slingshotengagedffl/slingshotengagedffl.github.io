Alex V. went 12-1 in the regular season, dubbed the Luck King for a campaign built more on favorable schedules than dominant performances. Jack's team was unspectacular by comparison — his MVP was DeVante Parker, a mid-season trade acquisition who had bounced through two other rosters before landing in Jack's lineup. Jack became the first back-to-back champion and the first owner to win two titles, while Alex V. pocketed the Luck King title and little else.

The two highest-scoring teams in league history played each other in the same championship game. Those records still stand eight years later. Kyle's squad set the all-time scoring record; Jack's finished second all-time. Kyle was the heavy favorite.
